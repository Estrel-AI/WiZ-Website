Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We're here to help.

Browse through our most frequently asked questions to find the answers you need about our platform, features, and pricing.

1. What is WiiZ?

WiiZ is a no-code orchestration platform for Agentic AI Orchestration. It's designed to help organizations visually build, deploy, and scale complex automations with simplicity and speed.

2. Who can use WiiZ?

WiiZ is built for a wide range of users, from enterprise teams to small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs). Whether you’re looking to automate complex back-office processes or build intelligent customer-facing agents, WiiZ provides the tools you need.

3. How does WiiZ work?

WiiZ works by providing a visual drag-and-drop canvas where you connect pre-built nodes representing data sources, AI models, and actions. You visually design your workflow, configure the nodes through a simple interface, and our platform handles the complex code and execution in the background.

4. Do I need technical skills to use WiiZ?

No. WiiZ is a no-code platform designed for both technical and non-technical users. Our intuitive interface allows you to build powerful workflows without writing any code. For advanced use cases.

5. Is my data secure with WiiZ?

Absolutely. Data security is fundamental to our platform. We offer hybrid deployment options, including on-premises, to keep your data within your environment. All connections and credentials are encrypted, and we follow industry best practices to ensure your workflows are secure and compliant.

6. How can I get access to WiiZ?

You can get started by exploring our subscription plans on the Pricing page or by requesting a personalized demo through our Contact page. Our team will get in touch to help you find the perfect solution for your needs.

7. What industries can benefit from WiiZ?

WiiZ is highly adaptable and provides value across many industries, including finance for automated reporting, customer service for intelligent ticket routing, and sales for marketing automation. Its flexibility makes it a powerful tool for any sector looking to leverage AI.

8. Can WiiZ integrate with other systems?

Yes. Integration is at the core of WiiZ. Our platform features a library of pre-built connectors for popular systems like CRMs, ERPs, and databases. For custom needs, you can easily connect to any service with a REST API.

9. How is WiiZ different from other solutions?

Unlike traditional automation tools that focus on simple, rule-based tasks, WiiZ is an AI-native orchestration platform. Our key differentiators are the visual drag-and-drop builder for complex agentic workflows, seamless integration of multiple AI models, and enterprise-grade security with hybrid deployment options.

10. How do I contact the WiiZ team?

You can reach us easily by visiting our Contact Us page and filling out the form. Our team is ready to assist you with any questions you may have.
